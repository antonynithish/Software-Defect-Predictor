Evaluation Metric Guide

Understanding the model performance measurements

Accuracy

Proportion of all predictions classified correctly.

Precision

Proportion of predicted defective modules that were actually defective.

Recall

Proportion of actual defective modules identified by the model.

F1 Score

Harmonic mean of precision and recall.

ROC-AUC

Measures ranking discrimination across classification thresholds.

PR-AUC

Summarizes the precision-recall trade-off, especially useful with imbalanced classes.

These metrics describe performance on the recorded evaluation dataset. They do not guarantee performance on unrelated software projects.
